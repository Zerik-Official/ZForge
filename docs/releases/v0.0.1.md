# ZForge 0.0.1

First beta of ZForge, a launcher for World of Warcraft 3.3.5a, 2.4.3 and 1.12 clients built on Tauri and WoWControlSDK.

## Highlights

- **Games** — add each client with its expansion, background video, logo and icon; pick the one to play from the home dock.
- **Game settings** — the launcher reads and writes the client's `Config.wtf`, with conflict detection when the game changed it.
- **Accounts** — save accounts by server, with the password in the Windows Credential Manager; log in, pick the realm and enter the world with a character, also in the background until the character is in the world.
- **Addons** — enable and disable addons by account or character, install them from a zip and delete them.
- **Discord Rich Presence** — the whole session, from the login and realm list to loading screens with their image and the zone in the world, with privacy options such as a random zone and a preview in the settings.
- **Notifications** — friends, whispers with a reply box, group invitations, summons, Dungeon Finder, trades, duels and battlegrounds, with the portrait of the other player.
- **Themes** — installable themes that change colors, fonts and the look of the controls.
- **Updates** — the launcher checks for new versions on startup and installs them in place; the SDK is verified against its published release.

## Requirements

- Windows 10 or 11 with WebView2.
- A World of Warcraft 3.3.5a, 2.4.3 or 1.12 client.
- Live game features (accounts, Rich Presence details, notifications) need a 3.3.5a client and WoWControlSDK, which the launcher installs.
